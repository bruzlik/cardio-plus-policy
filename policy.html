Key Summary: Cardio+ is an offline-first health journal. Your health records, medication schedules, laboratory tests, and biometric data are stored strictly on your local device. We do not sell your personal or medical data to third parties.

1. Introduction

Cardio+ ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and medical information is processed, collected, and protected when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Process

The App enables users to track personal health and wellness metrics. Depending on the modules you enable, the App may store locally on your device:

  • Hemodynamic Data: Systolic and diastolic blood pressure, pulse rate, arrhythmia indicators.
  • Pharmacotherapy: Medication names, dosages, intake schedules, and adherence logs.
  • Nephrology & Dialysis: Pre- and post-dialysis weight, ultrafiltration volume, dry weight targets, peritoneal dialysis logs.
  • Fluid Balance: Daily fluid intake and diuresis records, beverage categories, edema observations.
  • Laboratory Tests: Blood and urine biochemical markers, reference ranges, and derived medical indices (e.g., eGFR CKD-EPI).
  • Well-being and Activity: Sleep duration, step counts, general symptom logs.

3. Local Storage and Data Ownership (Offline-First)

All biometric and medical data entered into Cardio+ is stored locally on your device in an encrypted/isolated SQLite database (Android Room). You have full ownership and control of your data. The App remains fully functional without an internet connection.

4. Permissions Used by the Application

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to deliver timely, exact medication intake reminders and alarms chosen by the user.
  • BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (BLE): Used optionally and solely to connect with compatible certified blood pressure monitors and scales via the standard Bluetooth SIG Health Device Profile.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display medication alerts and health reminder notifications.
  • INTERNET: Used only if you explicitly enable cloud backup/sync or send crash reports. All transmission is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or user-tracking SDKs. We do not monetize or share your health records with any advertisers.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Cardio+ is a self-monitoring tool and health journal. It is not a certified medical device and is not intended for the acute diagnosis, cure, or treatment of medical emergencies. Always consult your physician or qualified healthcare provider before making any medical decisions or modifying your treatment plan.

7. Data Deletion and User Rights (GDPR / RODO Compliance)

Under EU GDPR / Polish RODO regulations, you have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. You can clear all data within the App by navigating to Profile → Delete All Data or by clearing application storage in Android system settings.
